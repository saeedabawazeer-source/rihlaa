# site-brief.md — Rahlaa (رحلاء)

- **Project name:** Rahlaa (رحلاء)
- **Name root & meaning:** Derived from "Rahl" (رحل — journey, explore, travel, roam) + "aa" suffix matching the brand family harmony (Nahlaa, Ajwaa). Meaning: "The Explorer's Gateway / رحلاء السفر والمغامرة".
- **OneWord (Baaghil model):** Wander (الترحال) — Effortless discovery of luxury stays, vacation rentals, and car rentals.
- **Niche/category:** Travel Affiliate & Direct Booking Comparison Engine (Hotels, Vacation Rentals / Airbnb alternatives, Car Hire).
- **Target audience:** Travelers, vacation seekers, digital nomads, and road-trippers looking for guaranteed price comparisons and direct bookings.
- **Content type/format:** Search aggregator, interactive directory, listing cards, map view, affiliate redirect engine.
- **Teal anchor shade (brand.md §3.2):** `#0B7A75` (Port canonical teal anchor color across the brand family).
- **Brand-specific accent:** Coral Amber `#FF7A00` (Rahlaa's unique energetic warm accent, reserved for primary CTA actions).
- **Base Canvas:** Paper Sand `#F6EFE3` (warm cream, near-black `#131110` text, `#000000` ink borders).
- **Typography:** `Space Grotesk` (Display/Headlines), `JetBrains Mono` (Labels/Data/Badges), `Cairo` (Arabic).
- **Monetization model:** Affiliate Commissions (Travelpayouts, Booking.com, VRBO, DiscoverCars, Agoda) + AdSlot Zones.
- **Launch target date:** Immediate live release.
